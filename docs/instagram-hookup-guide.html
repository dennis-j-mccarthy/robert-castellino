Connecting Instagram to the Castellino Photo ad account

Step-by-step, with a picture of each screen. About 20 minutes. Prepared for Bob Castellino by Dennis McCarthy, November 2026.

Before you start, have these ready

  • Your Instagram username and password. If Instagram uses a code sent to your phone, keep the phone next to you.
  • Be signed in to Facebook as yourself, on a computer, not the phone. You are the admin of the Page "Robert Castellino Photography, LLC".
  • Dennis on the phone if you want. He can see the same screens and will do the clicking in Parts B and C while you type the Instagram login.

Why this matters

The book ads are already showing on Instagram, but under a stand-in name that nobody can tap or follow. Once your real Instagram account is connected, the ads carry your handle, people can follow you from them, and we can post and boost from the same place.

In every picture, the red box is where you click.

Part A · on your phone · 3 minutesMake the Instagram account a Professional account

Ads can only run through a Business or Creator account. If yours already says "Professional dashboard" at the top of your profile, skip to Part B.

1

Open the Instagram app and go to your profile (the person icon, bottom right). Tap the three lines ☰ in the top right.

2

Tap Settings and activity, scroll to For professionals, tap Account type and tools, then Switch to professional account.

3

Choose Business, pick a category (type "Photographer"), and tap through the remaining screens with Next / Done. You can skip anything optional. Nothing on your profile changes except a new "Professional dashboard" button.

Part B · on the computer · 5 minutesConnect Instagram to the Facebook Page

The Page "Robert Castellino Photography, LLC" is what the ads run from. Linking Instagram to the Page is what gives the ads a real Instagram identity.

1

In a browser go to facebook.com. On the left, click Pages, then click Robert Castellino Photography, LLC. You are now "using" the Page; your profile picture at the top right becomes the Page's logo.

2

Click your Page picture in the top right, then Settings & privacy → Settings.

3

In the left column click Linked accounts. On the right, next to Instagram, click Connect account.

4

A box asks whether to allow Instagram messages in your inbox. Either answer is fine; click Confirm. An Instagram login window opens. Type your Instagram username and password and click Log in. If Instagram texts you a code, enter it.

5

Back on the Linked accounts screen, Instagram should now show your username and Connected. If it says "Switch to a professional account first", go back to Part A.

Part C · on the computer · 5 minutesAdd Instagram to the business portfolio and the ad account

Part B tells Facebook the Page and the Instagram account belong together. Part C tells the ad account it is allowed to spend through that Instagram account.

1

Go to business.facebook.com/settings. If it asks which business portfolio, choose Robert Castellino (not "The Book Works").

2

In the left column, under Accounts, click Instagram accounts. Then click the blue Add button.

3

Click Connect your Instagram account. The same Instagram login window as before appears. Log in again.

4

The next screen is the important one. It asks which ad accounts may use this Instagram account. Find Castellino Photo and switch its toggle on. Then click Next / Done.

5

If a "People" screen follows, switch on Dennis McCarthy as well so he can pick the account when building ads. Click Done.

Part D · 1 minuteCheck that it worked

1

Still in Business settings, click Ad accounts → Castellino Photo → the Connected assets tab. Your Instagram username should be listed.

2

Text or email Dennis: "Instagram is connected." He confirms it from his side and switches the ads to use your real handle at the next round.

If something gets in the way

  • "You don't have permission." You are signed in to Facebook as a profile that isn't the Page admin. Sign out and back in as yourself.
  • "This Instagram account is already connected to another Page." Instagram allows one Page per account. Disconnect it from the old Page first (Instagram app → Settings → Accounts Center → Accounts → remove), then redo Part B.
  • Instagram login loops or asks for a code you never get. Open the Instagram app on the phone; the code is often waiting there as a notification.
  • Can't find the business portfolio named Robert Castellino. At the top left of Business settings there is a dropdown of portfolios. Pick the one that is not "The Book Works".
  • Anything else: stop and call Dennis. Nothing here can break the running ads.
