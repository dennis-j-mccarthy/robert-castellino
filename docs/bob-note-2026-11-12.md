Bob,

Here's where the book ads stand after the first week, and how to see it all yourself.

What happened Nov 6 to 12
We spent $216.50, about $33 a day. The ads reached about 14,000 people and 2,910 of them clicked through to the book page, at 7 cents a click. That is a very cheap click. The problem is what happened next: 2,347 people looked at the book page, one started checkout, and there was one purchase. The ads were set to find people who click, and Meta found them. Most were 65 and over, scrolling Facebook, tapping a beautiful photo to see it bigger. They were not shopping.

Four of your plates clearly won: the red aspens (that image ran with two different headlines and took the most money), Mesa Verde, and Maroon Bells. The other 33 ads barely ran.

What changed on Nov 12
I started a second campaign, Book · Sales, at $25 a day. This one tells Meta to find people who buy, not people who click. It uses the three winning plates with the actual book cover set into the corner and the book facts next to it, so the ad reads as a book for sale, not just a photograph. The original campaign is cut to $10 a day and trimmed to the four winners, so it keeps feeding the retargeting audience and nothing else.

I also tested the book page and checkout on an iPhone and an Android phone. Both go straight to Stripe. The page is fine.

Spend next week
About $35 a day, roughly $245 for Nov 13 to 19. First real read on the sales campaign is Sunday, Nov 15. The only number that matters now is purchases. Expect far fewer clicks and a higher cost per click than last week. That is what it should look like.

How to see it yourself
1. Go to robertcastellino.com/admin/login and sign in with your usual admin email and password.
2. Then go to robertcastellino.com/creator/ads. Or from the creator page, Channels, then Paid social in the left column.

At the top you'll see a Phase 2 update with everything above, then Running now, which shows every live ad big, with the audiences it's in and its spend, clicks and purchases for the last 7 days, pulled from Meta every few minutes. Below that is the plan for the rest of the holiday season.

Nothing on that page spends money or changes anything unless you flip a switch, so look around all you like.
