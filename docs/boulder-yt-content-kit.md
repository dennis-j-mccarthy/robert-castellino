# Boulder: Yesterday & Today — social + email kit

Material pulled from Bob's book *Boulder: Yesterday & Today — A Photographic
Retrospective* (plus the companion "Brief History" and Q&A files). This is the
plan for turning it into local social posts and email. Everything below is drawn
from the actual book text and captions — no invented history.

Eight ready images live in `public/gift-series/boulder-yt/` (see that README for
credits and the resolution caveat — they're organic-only until we get hi-res
originals). The real engine here is the **content**, not the image count.

## Why this works
Boulder "then and now" is some of the best-performing local content there is.
People who live here, went to CU, or moved away all stop for a photo of a street
they know shot a hundred years ago. It's share-bait, it's community, and it keeps
Bob's name on the archive he's spent his career building. Lead with the history,
let the book ride along behind it.

## 1. The "Then / Now" series (the spine of this)

## 4. History hooks (all sourced from the book — safe to post verbatim-ish)
- Chief **Niwot (Left Hand)**, Southern Arapaho peacemaker, camped along Boulder
  Creek in Nov. 1858; the valley was a native wintering ground before the gold rush.
- The **Boulder City Town Company** formed **Feb. 10, 1859**.
- Town **incorporated Nov. 4, 1871**; the name was shortened from *Boulder City* to
  *Boulder*.
- **CU**'s board first met **Jan. 29, 1870**; **Old Main**, the first building, went
  up in **1877**.
- Two railroads reached town in **1873** (Colorado Central; Denver & Boulder Valley).
- **Chautauqua** turned 100 in **1998** and helped launch Boulder's open-space ethic.

**Quotes to drop into posts (attributed):**

## 6. Email angles
- **"Boulder Then & Now" monthly** — one pairing + a short story. Low effort, high
  open rate for a local list; the book is the footer CTA.
- **A history welcome** — new subscribers get the founding story (Niwot → Town
  Company → CU) over 2–3 sends, each ending on the book.
- **Book announcement / reprint** — straight "this exists, here's how to get it,"
  once we have a place to send people (see open questions).
