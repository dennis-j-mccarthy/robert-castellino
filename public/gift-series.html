Who we'll reach

Five audiences. All of them are specs only, and nothing gets created until Bob shares the account. Interest names change in Ads Manager, so check each one on day one and drop any that no longer exist.

A · Warm: Page and Instagram engagersCustomBook · prints
Definition
Anyone who engaged with the Facebook Page or the Instagram account in the last 365 days. That covers likes, comments, saves, shares, messages and profile visits. Combine both sources into one audience.
Why
These people already know Bob's work. They're the cheapest place to sell the book, and the best source for the lookalike.
Limits
Instagram engagers only work if the account is a business or creator account linked to the Page. If the audience comes in small, merge it with D rather than running it alone.
B · Lookalike of ALookalikeBook
Definition
1% lookalike of audience A, United States. Swap the source to purchasers once the pixel has logged enough sales.
Why
It finds new people who look like Bob's existing fans. That's the main way to reach people outside Colorado who'd want the book.
Limits
The source needs at least 100 people in one country. Meta increasingly treats lookalikes as a suggestion and widens them on its own, so judge it on cost per sale, not reach.
C · Cold: Colorado, landscape and homeSavedBook · prints
Definition
People living in Colorado, ages 30–65+. Interests (match any): landscape photography, fine-art photography, Rocky Mountain National Park, hiking, coffee table books, interior design, art collecting.
Why
The brief's core buyer: someone who lives here and wants the place in the house. This is the audience for prints as well as the book.
Limits
With Advantage+ audience switched on, interests are only a suggestion and Meta can go wider. Excluding interests is no longer available, so exclude A and E by audience list instead.
D · Cold: Colorado gift buyers, nationwideSavedBook (holiday)
Definition
United States, ages 35–65+. Interests (match any): Colorado, Denver, Rocky Mountain National Park, Aspen, Telluride, ski resorts. Narrow further (must also match): gifts, holiday shopping, or the "engaged shoppers" behavior. Runs Nov 3 – Dec 17.
Why
It reaches people who left Colorado, and family of Coloradans, at gift time. The brief's line for them: "Homesick? It's all in here."
Limits
"Former Colorado resident" can't be targeted directly; interest in Colorado is the closest available stand-in. Expect this to be the most expensive of the five.
E · Retarget: site visitors who didn't buyCustom (website)Book · prints
Definition
Visited /book or opened a print in the last 14 days, minus anyone who purchased in the last 30 days.
Why
The brief says to retarget for two weeks with the object ads only: the book on a table, a print on a wall, and the price.
Limits
It can't exist until the Meta Pixel is on robertcastellino.com, and it fills slowly at Bob's traffic levels. Start the pixel now, even before ads, so this audience is filling.

Site tracking · checklist for whoever manages robertcastellino.com

For whoever manages robertcastellino.com. Right now there's no Meta Pixel and no analytics on the site. Checkout runs through Stripe's hosted page, and buyers return to /checkout/success.

  • Add the Meta Pixel base code to the site layout (src/app/layout.tsx) so it loads on every page.
  • ViewContent on /book and when a print opens in the gallery. Include the product name and price.
  • InitiateCheckout when Buy is clicked, before going to Stripe (BookBuy.tsx, print-modal.tsx).
  • Purchase on /checkout/success, using the order total and currency from the Stripe session. Set event_id to the Stripe session ID.
  • Conversions API: add a Stripe webhook (checkout.session.completed) that sends the same Purchase to Meta from the server, with the same event_id so Meta doesn't count it twice. Send the buyer's email hashed, never in plain text.
  • Verify the robertcastellino.com domain in Bob's Meta Business settings, and set Purchase as the top-priority web event.
  • Add UTM tags to every ad link: utm_source=meta&utm_campaign=<set>&utm_content=<ad>.
  • If there's a real offer: create the coupon in Stripe and turn on promotion codes in checkout (src/app/api/checkout/route.ts).
  • Add a short privacy line saying the site uses the Meta Pixel.
  • Test with Meta's Test Events tool: one book purchase and one print purchase, each counted once.
